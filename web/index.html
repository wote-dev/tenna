What it does

Six things, done properly, instead of thirty half-finished ones.

  • Notifications land where you already look

    Alerts appear in macOS Notification Center with the app's real name and icon. Muted apps and foreground-service clutter never leave the phone.

  • Reply with a real keyboard

    Type into the notification on macOS and the text goes back through the app's own reply field — not a workaround, and not an SMS gateway.

  • Press the buttons the app shipped with

    Mark as read, archive, snooze, mute — whichever action buttons a notification carries are invokable straight from the Mac.

  • Dismiss it once

    Clear it on the Mac and it clears on the phone, and back again. You never triage the same alert twice.

  • One clipboard, both directions

    Copy a link, a code or an image on either machine and paste it on the other. Every image is checked against its advertised size and hash before it lands.

  • Stays between your two machines

    A single TLS connection runs directly between the apps. If your router blocks device-to-device traffic, Tennanova keeps a USB tunnel alive on its own.

Set up once, in about a minute

Three steps, then it is quiet forever.

  1. 1

    Open the Mac app

    It appears in the menu bar and shows a pairing code the moment it launches.

  2. 2

    Scan it on the phone

    Tap Scan pairing code. The scanner is the system one, so Tennanova never asks for your camera.

  3. 3

    Confirm two Android grants

    Notification access, then Accessibility for clipboard capture. Coming back from each Settings screen moves you along automatically.

Never: Shizuku, a daemon, wireless-debugging pairing, redoing anything after a reboot, or handing over an email address to use the app.

No cloud. No account. No telemetry. A single encrypted connection between your two machines — nothing is ever uploaded.
